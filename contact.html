Client FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about placing print orders with Shivba Graphics in Sinnar.

1. How fast can I get my visiting cards in Sinnar?

Standard and Matte laminated cards can typically be printed and ready for pickup within 24 to 48 hours. Express same-day service is also available for urgent business meetings.

2. Do I need to order in large quantities?

No! We have zero minimum order quantity (0 MOQ) on gifts like custom mugs, bottles, single-piece roll-up standees, and small batches of t-shirts.

3. What if I don't have a ready print design file?

You don't need a designer. Just provide your logo or text idea. Our in-house team creates the print mockup and sends you a digital proof on WhatsApp for approval before we print.

4. Do you provide GST invoices for companies in Sinnar MIDC?

Yes. We provide 100% compliant GST invoices for all corporate and enterprise purchases so you can claim your input tax credit.

5. What payment options are accepted?

We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), direct bank NEFT/RTGS, and Cash upon store pickup.
